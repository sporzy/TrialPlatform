## 17. £16 for Scafell Pike: why fell races stand apart (carosello, 4 slide)
1. £16. Scafell Pike and Great Gable included.
2. **Borrowdale Fell Race** · Lake District · 27 km, 2,000 m · £16 (2026, same as 2025) · 350 places, pre-entry only
3. Fell races are a different sport: often no waymarking, map and compass required (Borrowdale bans GPS for navigation), entry by experience, run by clubs and volunteers.
4. That's why we don't put them in the price comparison: they would make every other race look expensive. On Fair KM they have their own grey label, "category apart".

**Caption:**
> Borrowdale: 27 km, 2,000 m of climbing over Scafell Pike and Great Gable, for £16.
>
> It's a fell race, run by the Borrowdale Fell Runners club. Category AL: long and hard, with full waterproofs, map and compass required whatever the weather. First-timers must have finished two long fell races since 2024.
>
> We love fell races. But they're not the same product as a waymarked trail race with aid stations, medals and timing chips, so we keep them separate. Same for club races.
>
> Have you run Borrowdale? Tell us about it in the comments.

**Fonti:** https://www.sientries.co.uk/event/borrowdale-fell-race-2026 · https://www.sientries.co.uk/event/borrowdale-fell-race-2025 · https://www.borrowdale-fellrunners.org/races/borrowdale-fell-race/

**Note:** nessun costo per km-effort in slide (sarebbe £0,34): il post spiega proprio perché non lo mettiamo in classifica. L'anno di nascita della gara non è nel database: non citarlo senza verificarlo sul sito del club. Requisito per chi non l'ha mai finita: dal 01/01/2024 due gare FRA diverse di categoria A o B lunghe, oppure due A medie. "Medals and timing chips" è una descrizione generica delle gare trail, non di gare precise. Hashtag in più: `#fellrunning #lakedistrict`.
